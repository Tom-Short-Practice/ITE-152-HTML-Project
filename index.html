🌞 Black Holes 🌌

Space. We take it up. We put it in between words as we type – heck, there’s even a bar for it! No cocktails, however. And then there’s that big, black, inky void all around our little, blue marble of life. Who knows what's out there? That's what astronomers, astronauts, and astrophysicists aim to find out!

Space contains everything we can ever observe, and so much more. One of the most beautiful celestial bodies, and most terrifying, is black holes. Black holes, or quantum singularities, are areas of matter so dense that light can’t escape. But there’s a lot scientists don’t know about them. And there’s even more that the average person doesn’t know! For example, despite popular phrasing, did you know that our Sun isn’t going to explode into a black hole? And did you know that they don’t “explode” in the way we normally think, like a bomb? Join me on this journey of discovery to the most mysterious, and most intense, natural phenomenon in the universe!
